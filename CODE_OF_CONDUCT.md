# Code of Conduct

AgentPorter follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback on the work rather than the
person, and keep harassment, insults and personal attacks out of every project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@WebEferen](https://github.com/WebEferen) privately through
GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly do so.
